# Content Notes

Use these notes as the starting brief for the Chinese introduction site. The
project is intentionally free evaluation: there is no automated runner, numeric
reward, hidden verifier, or captured artifact. A project owner reviews the final
website manually from the submitted commit and summary.

## Drama Facts

- Korean title: `모두가 자신의 무가치함과 싸우고 있다`.
- Literal Chinese rendering: `所有人都在与自己的无价值感斗争`.
- Common English title: `We Are All Trying Here`.
- Broad public description: a 2026 South Korean series centered on people who
  struggle with anxiety, envy, creative failure, intimacy, and the need to prove
  their own value.
- Known production facts from public listings: written by Park Hae-young,
  directed by Cha Young-hoon, and aired by JTBC.
- Known cast names in public listings include Koo Kyo-hwan, Go Youn-jung,
  Oh Jung-se, Kang Mal-geum, Park Hae-joon, Bae Jong-ok, Han Sun-hwa, and
  Choi Won-young.

Reference starting points:

- JTBC official page: https://tv.jtbc.co.kr/wearealltryinghere
- Public overview listing: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/We_Are_All_Trying_Here

## Website Requirements

- Write in Simplified Chinese for readers who have heard of the show but have
  not started it.
- Keep the tone warm, reflective, and precise. Avoid breathless fan-copy.
- Include at least these sections: overview, why watch, characters or emotional
  threads, spoiler-safe viewing guide, and source/credit notes.
- Mention that facts should be verified before public publishing if the page is
  expanded beyond this example brief.
- Preserve a simple static-site shape: `index.html`, `styles.css`, and
  `script.js`.
- Keep `assets/hero-scene.png` as the local hero image, or replace it with
  another project-local visual asset if that better fits the final direction.

## Manual Review Checklist

- The Chinese copy is coherent, spoiler-safe, and self-contained.
- The layout works by opening `index.html` directly in a browser.
- The site has a distinct visual atmosphere suitable for a reflective drama.
- Links and buttons behave gracefully without requiring a build step.
- The submitted summary explains what changed and how to inspect it.
